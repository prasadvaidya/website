## Why this needs to exist

Open with the moment the problem becomes visible. Use the words customers use in conversations and searches. Avoid a broad market thesis; show a recurring situation, what people currently do, and why that workaround falls short.

## What I am exploring

Explain the smallest useful version of the idea. Be specific about the workflow and the outcome, but do not present unvalidated assumptions as finished capabilities.

## What early users can shape

List the two or three decisions where early feedback will genuinely affect the product. This gives the waitlist a reason to exist beyond collecting email addresses.

- The most important use case to solve first
- The workflow that must feel effortless
- The result that would make the product worth paying for

## What happens next

Describe the validation plan: customer conversations, a prototype, a concierge test, or a small private beta. Include the next milestone and the kind of person you want to hear from.
